为学日益，为道日损。损之又损，以至于无为，无为而无不为。取天下常以无事，及其有事，不足以取天下。In pursuing learning, one increases daily. In pursuing the Tao, one decreases daily. Decrease and again decrease, until one arrives at non-doing. Through non-doing, nothing is left undone. To take the world, one must always be without affairs; when one has affairs, one is not sufficient to take the world.——《道德经》第四十八章— Tao Te Ching, Chapter 48

老子怎么说What Lao Tzu says

第四十八章说为学日益，为道日损，损之又损，以至于无为——做学问是每天增加，修道是每天减少，减了再减，直到无为。老子把减当成一条正经的修行路，不是牺牲，是方法。Chapter 48 says in pursuit of learning you gain daily, in pursuit of the Way you lose daily. Lose and lose again until you arrive at non-doing. Lao Tzu treats subtraction as a serious practice, not a sacrifice. It's the method.
