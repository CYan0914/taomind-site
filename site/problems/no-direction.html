每条路看起来都对，也都不对Every path looks right and none of them do

别人都有目标，你连想成为什么样的人都答不上来。Everyone else seems to have a goal. You can't even answer what kind of person you want to be.

有物混成，先天地生。寂兮寥兮，独立不改，周行而不殆，可以为天下母。吾不知其名，字之曰道，强为之名曰大。大曰逝，逝曰远，远曰反。故道大，天大，地大，王亦大。域中有四大，而王居其一焉。人法地，地法天，天法道，道法自然。There is a thing, mixed and complete, born before heaven and earth. Silent and empty, it stands alone and does not change; it circles everywhere without danger, and can be the mother of the world. I do not know its name; I style it the Tao, and force a name on it: the Great. The Great means passing on; passing on means going far; going far means returning. Therefore the Tao is great, heaven is great, earth is great, and the king is also great. Within the realm there are four greats, and the king occupies one of them. Man follows earth, earth follows heaven, heaven follows the Tao, and the Tao follows nature.——《道德经》第二十五章— Tao Te Ching, Chapter 25

老子怎么说What Lao Tzu says

第二十五章说有物混成，先天地生，吾不知其名，强字之曰道——有个东西浑然一体，在天地之前就存在，我不知道它叫什么，勉强称它为道。连老子都说不知道名字，只能说强字之曰。说不清楚不等于没有，只是还没到能说清的时候。Chapter 25 says there's something formed in chaos, born before heaven and earth. I don't know its name, so I call it the Way. Lao Tzu himself says he doesn't know the name and is only approximating. Not being able to say it clearly doesn't mean it isn't there. It means it isn't ready to be said yet.

吾不知其名，强字之曰道I do not know its name; I call it the Way

今天可以做的一件事One thing to do today

写下三件最近让你忘了看时间的事。不看它有没有用，只看它让你忘了时间。Write down three things that recently made you lose track of time. Don't ask whether they're useful. Just notice which ones did it.
