躺下三小时，脑子还在开会It's 3 a.m. and your mind is still in a meeting

关灯之后，白天没处理完的事一件件冒出来。你越努力想睡，越清醒。Lights off, and every unfinished thing from the day surfaces one at a time. The harder you try to sleep, the more awake you get.

天下有道，却走马以粪；天下无道，戎马生于郊。祸莫大于不知足，咎莫大于欲得，故知足之足，常足矣。When the world has the Tao, swift horses are retired to haul manure. When the world lacks the Tao, war horses are bred at the borders. No calamity is greater than not knowing contentment; no fault is greater than the desire to acquire. Therefore the contentment of contentment is lasting contentment.——《道德经》第四十六章— Tao Te Ching, Chapter 46

老子怎么说What Lao Tzu says

《道德经》第四十六章说，祸莫大于不知足。睡不着的人，多半不是身体不累，是心里觉得还不够——今天的事没做完，明天的事没准备好，于是脑子拒绝关机。老子给的解法不是数羊，是把够这个标准还给自己。Chapter 46 says no disaster is greater than not knowing when you have enough. The problem is rarely that your body isn't tired. It's that something in you says today wasn't enough, so the mind refuses to shut down. Lao Tzu's fix isn't counting sheep. It's handing the standard of enough back to yourself.

知足者富Knowing when you have enough is wealth

今天可以做的一件事One thing to do today

睡前写下一件今天已经做完的事。只写一件，写完就停。Before bed, write down one thing you actually finished today. One. Then stop.
