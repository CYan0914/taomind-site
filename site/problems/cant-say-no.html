每次都答应，然后后悔，然后下一次还是答应。You agree, regret it, and then agree again next time.

善为士者不武，善战者不怒，善胜敌者不与，善用人者为之下。是谓不争之德，是谓用人之力，是谓配天古之极。One who is good as a warrior is not martial; one who is good at battle is not angry; one who is good at overcoming the enemy does not contend; one who is good at using people places himself below them. This is called the virtue of not contending; this is called the power of using people; this is called matching heaven, the utmost of antiquity.——《道德经》第六十八章— Tao Te Ching, Chapter 68

老子怎么说What Lao Tzu says

第六十八章说善为士者不武，善战者不怒，善胜敌者不与——真正会打仗的人不逞勇武，善于取胜的人不正面硬拼。老子说，最高明的赢，是根本不进入那场对抗。你不必赢下这次争执，你可以不进这场。Chapter 68 says a good warrior isn't violent, a good fighter doesn't get angry, a good winner doesn't engage. Lao Tzu's highest form of winning is never entering the contest. You don't have to win this argument. You can just not take it.

善战者不怒，善胜敌者不与The good fighter does not rage; the good winner does not engage
