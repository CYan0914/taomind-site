The multitude are bustling and happy, as if enjoying a great feast, as if ascending a terrace in spring. I alone am calm and without sign, like an infant not yet able to smile. 老子怎么说What Lao Tzu says

第二十章里有句话很有意思：俗人昭昭，我独昏昏；俗人察察，我独闷闷——别人都那么精明透亮，只有我糊里糊涂。这不是自嘲，是老子主动放弃了那场比赛。他不要那个昭昭，所以也不用承受它的焦虑。Chapter 20 has a strange line: other people are so bright and clear, I alone am muddled. Other people are so sharp, I alone am dull. It isn't self-pity. It's Lao Tzu walking away from the contest on purpose. He doesn't want the brightness, so he doesn't carry its anxiety either.
