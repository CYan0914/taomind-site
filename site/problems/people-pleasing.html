所有人的需求都满足了，除了自己Everyone's needs met except yours

你怕别人不高兴，于是每次都先委屈自己。You're afraid of upsetting people, so you keep taking the smaller share.

宠辱若惊，贵大患若身。何谓宠辱若惊？宠为下，得之若惊，失之若惊，是谓宠辱若惊。何谓贵大患若身？吾所以有大患者，为吾有身，及吾无身，吾有何患！故贵以身为天下，若可寄天下；爱以身为天下，若可托天下。Favor and disgrace are alarming; great trouble is honored as the body is. What does it mean that favor and disgrace are alarming? Favor is below; gaining it is alarming, losing it is alarming; this is what is meant by favor and disgrace being alarming. What does it mean that great trouble is honored as the body is? The reason I have great trouble is that I have a body; when I have no body, what trouble would I have? Therefore one who prizes the body as the world can be entrusted with the world; one who cherishes the body as the world can be given charge of the world.——《道德经》第十三章— Tao Te Ching, Chapter 13

老子怎么说What Lao Tzu says

第十三章说宠辱若惊，贵大患若身——被夸奖会惊，被羞辱也会惊。为什么？因为你把评价自己的权力交出去了。别人给，你就升；别人收，你就跌。老子说，那个惊的根源，是你太把我当回事了。Chapter 13 says favor and disgrace both disturb you, because you treat your self as something that can be given and taken. Praise lifts you, criticism drops you. Lao Tzu says the disturbance starts with how seriously you take the version of you that other people are grading.
