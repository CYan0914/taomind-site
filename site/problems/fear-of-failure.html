准备了一年了，还是没开始A year of preparation and you still haven't started

你怕的不是做不好，是怕证明自己不行。What scares you isn't doing it badly. It's finding out you can't.

上士闻道，勤而行之；中士闻道，若存若亡；下士闻道，大笑之，不笑不足以为道。故建言有之：明道若昧，进道若退，夷道若颣。上德若谷，大白若辱，广德若不足，建德若偷，质真若渝。大方无隅，大器晚成，大音希声，大象无形。道隐无名，夫唯道善贷且成。When the highest type of person hears the Tao, he diligently practices it. When the middle type hears it, he seems to keep it and seems to lose it. When the lowest type hears it, he laughs out loud. If it were not laughed at, it would not be the Tao. Thus the established sayings have it: the clear Tao seems dim; the advancing Tao seems to retreat; the level Tao seems uneven. The highest virtue seems like a valley; the greatest purity seems sullied; the broadest virtue seems insufficient; the established virtue seems remiss; the true and genuine seems to change. The great square has no corners; the great vessel is finished late; the great sound is rarely heard; the great image has no form. The Tao is hidden and nameless, and only the Tao is good at lending and at completing.——《道德经》第四十一章— Tao Te Ching, Chapter 41

老子怎么说What Lao Tzu says

第四十一章说上士闻道，勤而行之；中士闻道，若存若亡；下士闻道，大笑之——最好的听众听完立刻去做，中等的半信半疑，最差的大笑。注意，老子没提准备充分的人。真正的分水岭，是有没有开始走。Chapter 41 sorts listeners into three kinds: the best hear it and act immediately, the middle ones half-believe it, the worst laugh. Notice who's missing from that list. The well-prepared. Lao Tzu's dividing line isn't readiness. It's whether you start walking.
