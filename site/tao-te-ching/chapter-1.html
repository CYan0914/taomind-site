Tao Te Ching Chapter 1 · 道德经 第1章

The Tao that can be told is not the eternal Tao. The name that can be named is not the eternal name. Nameless is the origin of heaven and earth; named is the mother of ten thousand things.

白话精讲 · Plain-language Commentary

能说出来的道，不是永恒的道；能叫得出的名字，不是永恒的名字。一开口就“命名”，世界就开始分出你我、好坏、对错——可真正让万物生长的，往往在名字之外。

别急着给当下的人和事贴标签，先停一秒：这真的是它本来的样子吗？

Reflection

What cannot be named in your life right now? Where are you trying to define something that resists definition?
