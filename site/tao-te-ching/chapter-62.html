Tao Te Ching Chapter 62 · 道德经 第62章

The Way is the secret storehouse of all things, the treasure of the good, and the protection of the not-so-good. Fine words can buy respect; fine deeds can exalt others. How can the not-good be abandoned? Thus when the Son of Heaven is set up and the Three Dukes are appointed, though carved jade is offered before teams of horses, none is better than sitting and offering this Way. Why did the ancients value this Way? Did they not say: 'Seek and you shall find; if you have sinned, you shall be pardoned'? Therefore it is the most valued under heaven.

导读 · In brief

道是万物的隐秘仓库，对好人是宝藏，对普通人是庇护。再好的话也换不来真心，再好的事也比不上“行道”带来的踏实感——比如你加班到深夜，同事默默给你留了杯热咖啡，那一刻比任何口头赞美都更暖人心。 真正让人安心的，不是你多会说话、多能干，而是你心里有条路，走着走着，别人自然愿意跟着你。
