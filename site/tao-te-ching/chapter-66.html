Tao Te Ching Chapter 66 · 道德经 第66章

The great river and sea receive the homage of all streams because they lie lower than them. Thus the sage, to be above people, must speak as though below them. To lead them, must follow them.

白话精讲 · Plain-language Commentary

真正有格局的人，不是高高在上指挥别人，而是甘愿“低姿态”倾听、包容——就像大海不争高下，反而能汇聚百川。

你越想说服别人，越要先放下“我懂”的架子；真想带团队，先学会蹲下来听他们说。

Reflection

How can being 'lower' — more humble, more serving — increase your influence?
