Tao Te Ching Chapter 34 · 道德经 第34章

The great Tao flows everywhere, to the left and to the right. All things depend on it to live, and it does not turn away. It accomplishes its task but does not claim credit. It loves and nourishes but does not dominate.

白话精讲 · Plain-language Commentary

大道像空气一样无处不在，左右逢源，万物靠它生长却从不被它掌控；它默默做事、不邀功，滋养万物却不居高临下。

你不必总想着“我做了多少”，真正让人安心的，是做事时带着托底的温柔——比如帮同事改方案，不抢功劳，只把问题理清，对方自然愿意跟你长期合作。

Reflection

Where in your life can you 'flow everywhere' instead of following one rigid path?
