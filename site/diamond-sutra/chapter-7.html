Diamond Sutra Chapter 7 · 金刚经 第7品

Subhuti, what do you think? Does the Tathagata have any teaching to impart? Subhuti replied, 'No, World-Honored One. The Tathagata has no fixed teaching. For the Dharma cannot be spoken — it is not the Dharma, nor is it not the Dharma.'

白话精讲 · Plain-language Commentary

佛陀问须菩提：我有没有什么固定教法要传给你？须菩提答：没有，佛的教诲不是能用语言固定的“东西”，它既不能说“是”，也不能说“不是”——就像你没法把风抓在手里，但风确实存在。

落地启示：别把别人的话当铁律，也别把“道理”当标准答案；遇到问题时，先问问自己：“这说法，真能解决眼前这个具体问题吗？”

Reflection

What fixed teaching or belief are you holding, that a moment of true seeing would dissolve?
