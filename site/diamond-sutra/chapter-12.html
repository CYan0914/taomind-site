Diamond Sutra Chapter 12 · 金刚经 第12品

Moreover, Subhuti, wherever this sutra is spoken, or even where a single passage of it is recited, gods, men, and asuras should make offerings as though to the Buddha's stupa. How much more so for one who holds and recites the whole sutra.

白话精讲 · Plain-language Commentary

这经文说：哪怕只是念诵其中一小段，天上人间的众生都该像供奉佛塔一样恭敬对待——更别提完整持诵的人了。

你认真读完一篇有深度的文章、坚持学一项技能、甚至把某句道理用在具体决策上，别人未必懂你，但你心里那份笃定和踏实，就是最真实的“供佛”——它不靠仪式，靠你真正信了、用了、活出了。

Reflection

What place in your life deserves the reverence of a shrine — because truth lives there?
