Diamond Sutra Chapter 13 · 金刚经 第13品

What do you think, Subhuti? Can the Tathagata be seen by his thirty-two marks? 'No, World-Honored One.' 'Why? Because the thirty-two marks are not the thirty-two marks.' Subhuti, if anyone gave up body and life countless times, and another heard this sutra and had faith, the latter's merit would be greater.

白话精讲 · Plain-language Commentary

佛陀问须菩提：能靠三十二相认出佛吗？须菩提答：不能，因为那只是表象。

真正珍贵的不是外在标志，而是听闻此经后心生信解——哪怕舍身千次，不如此刻真心信受一句。

现代人常被“成功标准”绑架（比如职位、收入、颜值），但真正的力量不在别人眼里的“你该是什么样”，而在你是否愿意放下执念，听见自己内心那句“我信”。

Reflection

What outward sign of success or attainment are you mistaking for the real thing?
